# The economy of the First Bank of Friends

Every number here is either printed by a script in this repo or read from Robinhood Chain
(4663), and each is labelled **MEASURED** (read on chain or printed by a replay of the real
tape), **DERIVED** (arithmetic on measured inputs), **SYNTHETIC** (a generated tape that
shows the sign of an effect, never a prediction) or **CHOICE** (a parameter we picked, with
its consequence). The evidence tables are generated by `npm run economy` into
[`EVIDENCE.md`](EVIDENCE.md); the live figures are in `/api/desk`.

## 0. In one paragraph

A Rare Friend is paid every week in two currencies: RF, which comes from activations and
Reserve fees, and WETH, which is the 5% toll on every swap in the RF pool. Most holders
convert some of it, and today every conversion is a taker swap: it pays the 5% toll and
walks the Market's price curve, because the pool pays its liquidity providers nothing and so
nobody rests any liquidity. The bank turns members' conversions into **standing orders**:
single-sided range orders resting inside the pool. The bank never takes, so it never pays the
toll; the member is filled above the market instead of below it; and the counterparty on
every fill is an outside taker who pays 5% to every activated Friend, members included. The
bank does not bet on the price. It sells only what a member deposited to be sold, only above
the market, and it buys only below the last sale, only when the market is measurably
two-way. What it adds to the protocol is the thing the pool has never had: an order book.

## 1. The three facts it stands on (all MEASURED)

| fact | value | how it was read |
| --- | --- | --- |
| The pool pays makers nothing | `slot0.lpFee = 0`; the `fee` field of every Swap event is 0 | `npm run verify` |
| Takers pay 5%, in WETH, to Friends | `Hook.FEE_BPS() = 500`; `Hook.rewards()` is the ActivationManager | `npm run verify`, fork traces |
| The hook has no liquidity callbacks | FLAGS `0x20cc`; a range order pays no toll | `ForkDesk.t.sol: test_fork_AskFillsAsMakerWithNoHookFee` |

Together: **the whole distance between the market price and a resting order is free money
for whoever rests the order, and nobody does.** A taker who sells 1 RF receives at most
0.95 x mid, less impact. A resting ask one tick above the market fills at 1.018 x to 1.022 x
the spot at placement (MEASURED, median over every fill on the real tape). The gap is the
bank's edge and it does not depend on the price going anywhere: it is paid by the taker who
was going to trade anyway.

## 2. The loop

```
 every week, the protocol pays each activated Friend
   RF   (activations, hardwires, upgrades, Reserve fees)
   WETH (5% of every swap in the RF pool)
          |
          v   keeper claims when worth 20x gas; claim pays the Friend's own wallet
 +--------------------------------------------------------------------+
 | YOUR BOX in the bank: exact RF, exact WETH, no shares, no NAV      |
 |   RF you deposit   = a standing SELL order (maker, above market)   |
 |   WETH you deposit = a standing BID (maker, below the last sale)   |
 |                     ...only while the market is measurably two-way |
 +--------------------------------------------------------------------+
          |
          v   ONE ask and ONE bid rest inside the RF/WETH pool: the pool's first outside liquidity
   an outside TAKER crosses the bank's range
          |
          +---> the taker pays 5% in WETH to the ActivationManager
          |         -> streamed next week to EVERY activated Friend, by weight
          |         -> members get their share s of it back
          |
          +---> the proceeds land in the boxes of the members who funded that range,
                exactly, and leave whenever they ask
          |
          v   (pooled, designed, not yet in the contract)
   WETH buys a Genesis only under a floor-backed max bid; pooled RF pays the
   100,000 RF activation; weight compounds; the Reserve floor bounds the capital
```

## 3. Where the money comes from

| source | per unit | label | who pays it |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **The spread.** A resting ask fills above the market; a resting bid fills below it | fill / spot at placement = 1.018 to 1.022 (asks) | MEASURED on the real tape (`npm run economy`) | the taker who crossed |
| **The toll avoided.** A member converting through the bank pays 0% instead of 5% | +5.26% of the WETH leg (1 / 0.95) | MEASURED constant (`FEE_BPS`) | nobody: the bank is liquidity, not a swap |
| **The fee rebate.** Every taker who crosses the bank pays 5% to all Friends; members hold share s of all weight | 0.05 x s of every WETH crossed, one stream later | DERIVED from `totalWeight` | the taker |
| **The Genesis desk** (designed, not in the contract). A Genesis bought under the max bid earns its stream with capital recoverable at the Reserve floor | positive only under `genesis.maxBidUsd` in `/api/desk` | DERIVED, idle above the max bid | the reward stream |

Per unit converted, the first two rows together are the number that matters: **a median
+8.2% against the taker route at the moment of the decision** (MEASURED over every fill on the
real tape; 1.02 / 0.95 gives +7.4% before impact, DERIVED). It is execution, not prediction:
the member decided to convert; the bank only changed the route.

What the bank does **not** earn from: LP fees (there are none), wash volume (a round trip
of V WETH costs members 0.0975 V (1 - s) and is never positive; see `ECONOMICS.md` section 3),
a separate venue (it would divert every Friend's fee and see only arbitrage flow), or
leverage (there is no borrow path).

## 4. Where the RF goes (the part paired with $RAREFRIENDS)

- **Every fill routes 5% of its WETH leg to every activated Friend.** The bank's resting
  orders make that fill cheaper for the taker than walking the Market's curve at size, so the
  bank adds depth; the takers add the volume. We do not claim the bank adds volume.
- **RF sold by the bank is sold above the market, never through it.** A taker sale walks
  the price down; a resting ask waits for a buyer. The status quo (holders dumping claimed RF
  as takers) is replaced by an order book above the price. That is what "turning sell
  pressure into an order book" means, and it is the protocol-level reason the bank exists.
- **RF bought by the bank** (the grid's bids, when armed) is bought below the last sale,
  with WETH that the protocol paid members. That is structural bid for RF funded by swap
  fees: fees -> WETH rewards -> resting bids -> RF.
- **Activation is an RF sink the bank feeds.** Each Genesis the pooled desk would buy needs
  100,000 RF to activate: 50,000 burned, 50,000 to the RF reward stream (MEASURED, fork
  trace). Pooled RF pays it in kind, without a taker swap.
- **`allocate()` keeps everyone's stream running.** It is permissionless, it has been called
  once since launch, and the keeper is built to call it when due, for every Friend, member or not.

## 5. The rules that make it not lose

| rule | where it is enforced | what it prevents |
| --- | --- | --- |
| The bank never swaps; it only rests ranges | `RangeDesk.sol`, no swap path exists; `test_fork_AskFillsAsMakerWithNoHookFee` | paying the toll; the keeper dumping the book into a sandwich |
| Never sell RF the desk bought below cost x 1.05 | on chain: the loss lock, size-weighted cost basis | selling a bought inventory at a loss (a 5%/30-day loss budget exists; the keeper never spends it) |
| Never bid above the last sale x 0.95 for 30 days | on chain | buying back what was just sold, higher |
| Every range at least 1% clear of a time-weighted price anyone can update | on chain: `PoolObserver` | a keeper quoting against a spot it just moved |
| One ask and one bid, each 1% to 15% of its side, 50% of a side per day, 24 changes a day | on chain | churn, dust, a keeper spending the book on gas |
| Bids only when the market has swung 5% and back six times in 72h, trended under 10%, and last week replayed beats holding | keeper, `lib/strategy.mjs` | buying into a slide (every bid policy lost 20% to 30% from Sep 17, MEASURED) |
| Asks (the standing sell order) run whenever RF is deposited and worth its gas; in a rally they move to a take-profit range | keeper, `lib/strategy.mjs` `standingOrder` | selling the whole book at the edge into a rally (SYNTHETIC +5%/day: -49% vs holding with the brake, -54% without; the brake limits, it does not remove, and a member who wants to hold RF keeps it out of the box) |
| Withdraw any time, no owner check, no pause, no queue | on chain: `invariant_EveryoneCanExit` | anyone being trapped |

What it does not claim: **it does not beat holding RF in a rally.** A member who deposits RF
has asked for it to be sold; if RF doubles the next day they sold some of it at +2% instead
of +100%. The bank's promise is per unit converted, against the route the member would
otherwise have taken, and that promise holds in every window measured.

## 6. Your deposit is your instruction

There is no strategy switch to sign. The contract already gives every member a daily cap
per asset at signup (`join(capRfPerDay, capWethPerDay)`), and the bank reads those caps as
policy:

| you want | you do | what happens |
| --- | --- | --- |
| keep your RF | RF cap 0 (or withdraw RF from your box, any time) | RF stays in your Friend's wallet; the bank never touches it |
| sell your RF above the market | RF cap > 0 | your RF sits in the bank's one ask, pro rata with every other member's, and is sold only above the market |
| hold WETH | WETH cap 0, or leave it idle | nothing quotes it; it sits in your box |
| buy RF below the market when the market is two-way | WETH cap > 0 | your WETH joins the bank's one bid, only while the grid's rule is met |

A range's proceeds go only to the members who funded it, exactly, so a member who joins
later or leaves earlier is never paid from anyone else's fill (`test_AskProceedsGoOnlyToTheHoldersWhoFundedIt`).

Against **holding**, the result is the market's direction and nothing else: +71% over the real tape's whole life (RF fell 89%), about -0.3% in the flat last 72 hours, -49% in a synthetic +5%/day rally (the take-profit brake limits that only a little: -54% with it off). A sell programme trails holding when the asset keeps rising, whatever the execution; that is why converting at all is the member's instruction, not the bank's bet.

Conversion demand exists: over the tape, 372M RF was sold into the pool (58% of gross volume by WETH; the daily sell share ran 30% to 93%). Every unit of it went through a taker swap, paid 5% and ate impact. The standing order is the same flow, resting instead.

## 9. What comes next, in order

1. **Per-member sell / keep flag on chain.** Today "keep" means an RF cap of 0. A flag would
   let a member deposit RF and still keep it out of the ask. Contract change, after the audit.
2. **The Genesis desk on chain.** The bands are published in `/api/desk` today and idle while
   the market is above the max bid; the pooled buy, the in-kind activation and the NAV units it
   needs are designed in `ECONOMICS.md` section 6 and the 2026-09-22 quant report.
3. **Every RF-paired pool.** Rare Friends' own roadmap is token economies paired with RF. Each
   will have the same hook design: a toll on takers, no LP fee, no makers. The bank's desk is
   pool-agnostic (the pool key is a constructor argument, checked at deploy), so the same
   standing orders can rest in any of them.
4. **For the Rare Friends team.** A non-zero LP fee, or a share of the hook fee to liquidity in
   range, would make every holder a potential maker and deepen the pool at no cost to takers.
   The Laffer table in `ECONOMICS.md` shows a 1%/side venue needs 5x the volume to keep Friends
   whole; a fee share to liquidity needs none.

## 10. What we will not claim

- We will not say the bank adds volume. It adds depth; takers add volume.
- We will not quote a spread as yield without the direction risk beside it.
- We will not annualise a six-day-old protocol's reward stream.
- We will not publish a replay without the counterfactual beside it.
- We will not deploy the contract to hold anyone's money before an external audit.
