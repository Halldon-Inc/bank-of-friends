# The economy of the First Bank of Friends

Every number here is printed by a script in this repo or read from Robinhood Chain (4663), and each is labelled
**MEASURED** (read on chain, or printed by a replay of real trades), **DERIVED** (arithmetic on measured inputs),
**SELECTED** (a parameter picked by a test, and which test), **SYNTHETIC** (a generated tape that shows the sign of
an effect, never a forecast) or **CHOICE** (a parameter we picked, with its consequence).

- The swap desk's evidence: [`TAKER.md`](TAKER.md), generated by `npm run taker -- --write`.
- The live forward test on 22 pools across 7 launchpads: [`PAPER.md`](PAPER.md) and `data/paper/report.md`.
- The live figures: `/api/desk` (the `taker` block is the desk's decision this hour).

## 0. In one paragraph

A Rare Friend is paid every week in two currencies: RF (from activations, hardwires and Reserve fees) and WETH (the
5% toll on every swap in the RF pool). The bank does four things with it. It **harvests** each member's rewards into
their own safe deposit box, in kind. It runs a **swap desk** that trades the pooled RF and WETH through the pool when a
move is big enough to pay, **paying the full 5% toll on the way in and on the way out**. It **gets part of that toll
back**, because the toll is what every activated Friend is paid: members holding share s of all Friend weight receive
s of every toll the desk pays, the following week. And it pays everyone out **in kind, any time, with a receipt**:
what you deposited, what the desk added or cost, and your toll rebate, per asset.

## 1. The loop

```
 every week the protocol pays each activated Friend
   RF   (activations, hardwires, upgrades, Reserve fees)
   WETH (5% of every swap in the RF pool)
          |
          v  the keeper claims when it is worth 20x the gas; a claim always pays the Friend's own wallet
 +---------------------------------------------------------------+
 | YOUR BOX: exact RF and exact WETH, no shares                  |
 +---------------------------------------------------------------+
          |
          v  the pooled book
 +---------------------------------------------------------------+
 | THE SWAP DESK, hourly                                         |
 |   buy RF with WETH when RF is 30% under its 24h average,      |
 |       unless it is down 25% over 72h (a collapse, not a dip), |
 |       and never past 70% of the book in RF                    |
 |   sell RF for WETH when RF is 30% over its average,           |
 |       and only if the sale clears its cost after both tolls   |
 |   half of the idle side per trade; most hours it waits        |
 +---------------------------------------------------------------+
          |
          v  a real swap through the pool: the desk pays 5% of the WETH leg, in and out
   the ActivationManager -> streamed next week to EVERY activated Friend by weight
          |
          +--> members get back their share s of the desk's own toll (the rebate)
          |
          v
   RECEIPT, any time: deposited | swap desk result | toll rebate | coming home, per asset
```

## 2. Why a member-owned swap desk is different

Anyone can swap RF and pay 5% each way. What only a bank of Friends can do is **pay the toll to itself**.

| fact | value | label |
| --- | --- | --- |
| The toll on every swap | 5% of the WETH leg, both directions | MEASURED `Hook.FEE_BPS() = 500`, real receipts |
| Where it goes | the ActivationManager, streamed over the next 7 days to every activated Friend by weight | MEASURED `Hook.rewards()`, fork traces |
| A round trip of V WETH pays | 0.05 V + 0.05 x 0.95 V = 0.0975 V | DERIVED |
| Members get back | s of it, where s is their share of all Friend weight | DERIVED |
| So a round trip costs members | 0.0975 V (1 - s) | DERIVED |

| if the bank holds this share of all Friend weight | toll per round trip | swing a round trip must capture |
| ---: | ---: | ---: |
| 0.2% (one Genesis, the founding member today) | 9.73% | 10.78% |
| 10% | 8.78% | 9.62% |
| 25% | 7.31% | 7.89% |
| 50% | 4.88% | 5.12% |
| 75% | 2.44% | 2.50% |
| 90% | 0.97% | 0.98% |

**Every member who joins makes every other member's trades cheaper.** That is the network effect of this economy,
and it is paired with $RAREFRIENDS by construction: the rebate exists only because the pool pays its toll to Friends.

## 3. The rules (SELECTED, and how)

`lib/strategy.mjs` `TAKER` and `takerDecision`. One pure function decides for the backtests, the live paper test and
`/api/desk`, and `scripts/taker.mjs` checks that the shipped function reproduces its backtest exactly (32 of 32 runs).

| rule | value | how it was chosen |
| --- | --- | --- |
| band | trade only 30% away from the 24h average | SELECTED: 252 settings run on the FIRST half of 16 real pools with a 4% to 6% toll; the best 25th percentile (a worst-case-aware rule) won; then scored on the second halves |
| size | half of the idle side per trade | SELECTED, same test |
| collapse guard | no buying while the price is 25%+ below where it was 72 hours ago | SELECTED, same test |
| inventory cap | no buying past 70% of the book in RF | SELECTED, same test |
| profit lock | sell only if the proceeds after both tolls and impact beat the RF's cost; re-buy only below the last sale | CHOICE: "trade only when it makes money after the toll" |
| gas | $0.033 a swap | MEASURED |

## 4. The evidence, good and bad

**Out of sample: 16 real pools with a 4% to 6% toll, second halves** (MEASURED on their hourly history; the desk was
picked on the first halves only):

| the toll comes back at | vs holding, median | beat holding | 1 in 4 pools did worse than | worst | best |
| ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| 0% (those pools: their toll goes elsewhere) | -0.6% | 7 of 16 | -29.5% | -77.3% | +91.1% |
| 50% (what-if: the RF structure) | +6.2% | 9 of 16 | -27.8% | -75.6% | +98.8% |
| 90% (what-if: nearly every Friend banks here) | +11.6% | 9 of 16 | -22.3% | -74.2% | +105.0% |

**On RF's own history** (MEASURED, 10,012 swaps, 9.4 days, price -89%): starting at launch, the desk bought once in the
first hour and lost 35% against holding as RF fell. Started after the first 24 hours, as the other pools are run, it
never found a dip that was not a collapse and did not trade (+0.00%).

**Synthetic regimes** (SYNTHETIC, RF's pool depth, 3 seeds): chop and 4x-volume chop never moved 30% from the average,
so it waited; a -5%/day slide +62% to +66% against holding (it bought dips and sold the bounces); a +5%/day rally
-13% to -15% (it sold into the climb).

**Live, no money** (`docs/PAPER.md`): the same function on 22 live pools across 7 launchpads since 2026-09-26. It waits
most hours by design; its trades so far are listed there with every decision.

**Reading it honestly.** The desk is a swing trader with a strict rule set. On young 4% to 6% toll tokens it beat
holding about half the time out of sample, its median result improves with every point of toll that comes back, and
one pool in four still lost more than 20% against holding. It is not a guaranteed profit, and we do not claim one.
What is structural, and belongs only to Rare Friends, is the rebate: the break-even swing falls from 10.8% to 5.1% as
membership goes from one Genesis to half of all weight.

## 5. Where the RF goes (paired with $RAREFRIENDS)

- **Every desk swap routes 5% of its WETH leg to every activated Friend.** The desk's volume is real volume, and its
  toll is real rewards, including to Friends who never joined the bank.
- **The desk buys RF only on large dips that are not collapses**, with WETH the protocol paid members in swap fees:
  fees -> rewards -> dip bids -> RF.
- **It sells RF only above cost after both tolls**, so the bank is never a forced seller.
- **`allocate()` keeps everyone's stream running.** It is permissionless, it had been called once since launch, and
  the keeper calls it when due, for every Friend.
- **Activation is an RF sink the bank can feed**: each Genesis a future pooled desk buys needs 100,000 RF to activate
  (50,000 burned, 50,000 to the RF stream).

## 6. Your box and your receipt

- **Sign once**: from your Friend's own wallet, approve RF, approve WETH, `join` with a daily cap per asset. The NFT
  never moves, and your personal wallet approves nothing.
- **Auto-deposit**: the keeper harvests your Friend's rewards into your box, only what its own claim delivered.
- **The bank does its thing**: your box's RF and WETH are part of the pooled book the swap desk trades.
- **Claim any time**: withdraw RF, WETH or both, or close the account in one step, with no owner check and no pause.
- **Receipt**: deposited (your harvested rewards), swap desk (what its trades added or cost, per asset), toll rebate
  (your share of the toll the desk paid), coming home (the sum, in kind).

| Receipts | the ledger already keeps exact RF and WETH per member, so deposited and coming home are exact; the rebate line needs the harvest to tag the desk's share of each WETH claim |

## 8. What we will not claim

- We will not call the desk guaranteed profit. Out of sample it beat holding on 7 to 9 of 16 pools.
- We will not annualise a young protocol's reward stream.
- We will not publish a backtest without the counterfactual and the losing pools beside it.
- We will not deploy the contract to hold anyone's money before an external audit.

## Appendix: what else we tested

Before the swap desk we measured the alternatives, and they are kept in the repo as research: resting maker orders
instead of swaps (`docs/EVIDENCE.md`, `npm run economy`: fills at about 1.02x the spot at placement, +8% per unit sold
against a taker), a two-sided range grid (`docs/STRATEGY.md`), passive liquidity and self-volume (`docs/ECONOMICS.md`:
passive liquidity lost 42% to 55%). The product is the swap desk because it trades both sides and pays the toll like
every other trader, and the toll it pays comes back to its own members.
