# Backtest: the taker-side study of $RAREFRIENDS

Generated by `node scripts/backtest.mjs` against the complete swap history of the RF/WETH
pool (refreshed 2026-09-22, 8,826 swaps). Re-run it yourself; nothing here is hand-typed.

**Read this first.** This is the study that ruled market making OUT as a taker, and it is
kept because it is why the live desk is maker-only (see `docs/STRATEGY.md`). Two rows
below are not achievable and are labelled so:

- **S2 at a 4% spread shows a large gain. It is not real.** S2 assumes the bank "wins any
  fill it quotes", but nothing routes a taker to a quote outside the pool: the Market
  router and the rarefriends UI trade only the hooked pool. The only way to be filled is
  to be liquidity INSIDE the pool, which is what `scripts/backtest-gated.mjs` models.
- **S3 (crossing network)** assumes the same capture, and its revenue is fees diverted
  from every Friend's reward stream (the line marked "honest cost to the protocol").

Passive full-range LP (S1) is the realistic row, and it lost 43% to 55% vs holding.

Read it this way: a venue charging 1% per side must bring 5x today's volume for
Friend holders to end up no worse off. Whether a 10% -> 2% round-trip cost actually
multiplies turnover by 5x is an EMPIRICAL question this dataset cannot answer.
It is a hypothesis with a mechanism and a clear break-even, not a projection.
